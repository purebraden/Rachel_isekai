# Book 1 Canon Reference -- Thornhaven & Political Baseline

This document captures confirmed, on-page canon from Book 1.\
It serves as continuity lock for Book 2 development.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# POLITICAL BASELINE (End of Book 1)

## Council Status

-   Rachel battled Vex directly prior to the summons.
-   Vex warned Rachel that the Council would summon her.
-   The Council meeting occurred.
-   Vex was absent.
-   Rachel attended with Sivara.
-   The Council questioned her anomaly.
-   No sanction was issued.
-   No endorsement was granted.
-   Rachel was classified as "under observation."
-   Thornhaven retained autonomy.
-   No formal censure against Sivara.

## Vex Status

-   Acted independently of the Council.
-   Sent assassins after Rachel.
-   Engaged Rachel directly in battle.
-   Withdrew strategically.
-   Rivalry unresolved.

## Thornhaven Status

-   Stable at end of Book 1.
-   Aware of Rachel's anomaly.
-   Politically cautious.
-   Sivara respected but observed.

# CORE CHARACTERS

## Rachel

**Role:** Anomalous boundary walker\
**Core Trait:** Instinctive structural intuition\
**Emotional Core:** Belonging without surrender

### Established Behaviors

-   Senses boundary instability before intellectual understanding.
-   Acts under pressure before seeking permission.
-   Protects people over doctrine.
-   Pushes power beyond safe limits when necessary.
-   Bonds deeply with chosen family.
-   Resists surrender.

### Power Canon

-   Stabilizes major collapses beyond training.
-   Detects hinge distortions others cannot clearly see.
-   Communicates with boundary entities.
-   Warmth begins in palms, rises with exertion.
-   Overuse destabilizes her personal boundary alignment.
-   Broke free from summoning circle under extreme strain.
