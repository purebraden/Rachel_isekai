## RECOMMENDATION

**YES, with revisions the outline has enough conflict to avoid stalling.**

**Key changes needed:**
1. ✅ Remove "daily life" as category - integrate character moments INTO conflict scenes
2. ✅ Every "training/growth" chapter needs active problem to solve
3. ✅ Add social conflicts in Act II to vary pressure types
4. ✅ Increase personal stakes (attack, departure decision)
5. ✅ Make Council restrictions create immediate problems, not just tension
6. ✅ Character development happens DURING conflict, not in separate quiet chapters

**Conflict Rhythm Should Be:**
- Chapters 1-11: Building (✅ done)
- Chapters 12-25: Sustained pressure with variety
- Chapters 26-35: Escalating toward breaking point
- Chapters 36-43: Continuous crisis to climax

**Avoid:**
- Multiple consecutive chapters of same conflict type
- "Breathing room" chapters without tension
- Training montages
- Pure exposition or introspection without stakes

**Instead:**
- Vary conflict types chapter to chapter
- Character moments happen in crisis
- Training has consequences/problems
- Introspection driven by specific events

---

## REVISED PACING PRINCIPLE

**Every chapter needs at least ONE of these:**
1. Active threat (physical danger)
2. Choice with consequences (moral/strategic dilemma)
3. Revelation that changes understanding (mystery/discovery)
4. Relationship shift (alliance, betrayal, deepening)
5. Setback or complication (things get worse)

**No chapter should be pure:**
- Description
- Training
- Conversation without tension
- Setup without payoff

---

This structure should prevent stalling while maintaining Book 1's length and depth.
