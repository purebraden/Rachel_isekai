**Architectural realm. Slow time. Sparse presence.**

-   Sparse light, sourceless and eternal

-   Out of place but anchored by Rachel
-   His shadow-nature adapts but struggles
-   Protective instinct heightened
-   Awed by what Rachel is

Not threatening.

Not surprised.

Just... hoping.

**Recognition.**

Not of face (she's never met him).

Of resonance.

Family resonance.

Architectural frequency matching hers.

He sees her.

Understands immediately what she is.

Hybrid. Daughter.

**His expression:**

Hope.

Desperate hope.

Grief.

Love.

He thought she'd come back.

He's been waiting.

**Rachel realizes:**

Her father is asking about her mother.

He doesn't know Rachel exists.

He's been waiting for Theryn to return.

And instead, their daughter has found him.
