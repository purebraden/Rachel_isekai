# BOOK 2 -- TONE & VOICE RULE SHEET

## Series Continuity Document (Prose Locked Version)

This document ensures Book 2 matches the tonal, stylistic, emotional,
and prose framework established in Book 1.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# I. CORE TONE PRINCIPLES

## 1. Grounded Mythic

The series operates in mythic cosmology but grounded emotional realism.

-   No melodrama.
-   No prophecy-heavy exposition.
-   No cosmic monologuing.
-   Emotional beats are intimate, not theatrical.

When scale increases, prose tightens --- it does not become grandiose.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Emotional Restraint Over Spectacle

Even during high magic events:

-   Characters respond first as people.
-   Awe is quiet.
-   Fear is contained.
-   Love is shown through action and steadiness.

Avoid: - Overwritten reactions - Dramatic proclamations - Destiny
language

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. Political Fantasy with Intimacy

Political tension exists, but conversations remain personal and precise.

Council scenes: - Controlled - Formal - Strategic

Thornhaven scenes: - Warm - Lived-in - Interpersonal

Never allow politics to become abstract exposition.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# II. PROSE RULES (LOCKED)

## 1. Stephen King--Messy Energy

Book 2 must preserve the lived-in, slightly messy narrative energy of
Book 1.

This means:

-   Let scenes breathe.
-   Let characters think mid-action.
-   Allow awkward pauses.
-   Allow imperfect reactions.
-   Avoid clean cinematic beats.

The prose should feel inhabited, not engineered.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. No Staccato Overload

Avoid excessive short, punchy fragments.

Do NOT stack multiple two- or three-word sentences in a row unless under
extreme stress.

Instead:

-   Mix short sentences with longer ones.
-   Use clauses.
-   Let thoughts unwind naturally.
-   Allow sentences to stretch slightly before landing.

Rhythm should feel like natural speech, not drum beats.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. Vary Sentence Length Intentionally

Each paragraph should contain a blend of:

-   Longer, clause-driven sentences
-   Mid-length grounding sentences
-   Occasional short emphasis lines

The contrast creates texture.

Uniform sentence length is forbidden.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4. No Thesis Statements

Avoid summary conclusions such as:

-   "That mattered."
-   "This changed everything."
-   "She understood now."
-   "It was different."

Let readers infer significance through scene and reaction.

Show consequence through behavior.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 5. No Scene Summaries

Do not summarize emotional turning points.

Do not explain what the scene accomplished.

Do not narrate interpretation.

Events must unfold in real time.

If something matters, it will show in:

-   Physical sensation
-   Dialogue subtext
-   Changed dynamics
-   Silence

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 6. Avoid Authorial Commentary

No omniscient moralizing. No "little did she know." No future foreshadow
statements.

Stay close to character perception.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7. Dialogue Rules

-   Dialogue should feel natural and restrained.
-   No exposition speeches.
-   Characters do not explain cosmology unless necessary.
-   Interruptions and unfinished sentences are allowed.

Subtext over explanation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# III. CHARACTER VOICE RULES

## Rachel

Observational. Spatial thinker. Competent without arrogance.

Her internal narration may include:

-   Pressure
-   Angles
-   Alignment
-   Density
-   Time stretching

But never direct explanation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Arathen

Steady. Intentional. Expresses emotion through action.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Sivara

Disciplined. Formal in public. Direct in training.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Ilyra

Blunt. Instinctive. Protective through action.

Never comic relief.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Edrin

Pattern-oriented. Curious. Avoid exposition dumps.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Maelin

The nurturer and anchor.

She:

-   Notices exhaustion first.
-   Forces rest.
-   Places food in hands before arguments start.
-   Grounds tension physically and emotionally.

Her power is maintenance and care.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Vex

Measured. Patient. Never theatrical.

In dream-space:

-   Controlled
-   Observational
-   Dangerous through restraint

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# IV. MAGIC PRESENTATION RULES

-   No spell lists.
-   Magic shown through sensation and consequence.
-   Rachel's divergence manifests as:
    -   Realignment
    -   Rotation
    -   Redirection
    -   Structural editing

Escalation through complexity, not size.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# V. COSMOLOGY REVEAL RULES

-   No mention of angels before crossing.
-   Use myth fragments only.
-   Recognition before explanation.
-   Emotional connection before cosmology.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# VI. RELATIONSHIP RULES

Rachel and Arathen:

-   Grow together.
-   No jealousy tropes.
-   Tension from pressure, not mistrust.
-   Partnership under strain.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# VII. ENDING TONE RULE

Final sealed realm scene must be:

-   Quiet
-   Sparse
-   Slow
-   Intimate

No exposition. No grandeur.

Recognition. Hope. Question.

Cut.
