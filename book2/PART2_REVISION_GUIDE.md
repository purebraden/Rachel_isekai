## Prompting Strategy

For each revision, include in your prompt:

1. The scaffolding v3 section for that chapter (paste it directly)
2. The revision prompt from this document
3. The previous chapter's text (so ChatGPT has continuity)
4. The voice/tone rules from `Book_2_Tone_and_Voice_Rules_v3.md`
5. A reminder: "Do NOT introduce new terminology, new archive discoveries, or new institutional procedures. Follow the outline."

The key insight: **ChatGPT drifts toward what's easy to generate (dialogue, archives, politics) and away from what's hard (action, dream sequences, surreal imagery).** Your prompts must explicitly demand the hard scenes.
