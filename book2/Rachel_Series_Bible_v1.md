# SERIES BIBLE

## Core Mythology & Long-Term Cosmology Framework

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# I. COSMOLOGICAL FOUNDATION

## The Astronomical Wrongness

Long before current demon civilization stabilized, the universe
experienced a structural instability--- an astronomical wrongness in the
fabric of reality itself.

This wrongness was not moral or evil. It was architectural.

The Angels (true name TBD) were not holy beings but architectural
entities--- beings of structure, geometry, and boundary design.

They corrected the instability at catastrophic cost.

To fix reality, they: - Sealed their realm off from all others - Bound
the wrongness behind structural architecture - Sacrificed most of their
kind to stabilize the universe

The seal held.

But not perfectly.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# II. THE ANGEL REALM

-   Exists in a separate plane entirely.
-   Time moves significantly slower there.
-   The realm is sealed and inaccessible to demons.
-   Architecture is physicalized geometry.
-   Sparse population---only a few angels remain.
-   They sustain the seal from within.

They are mostly extinct --- but not entirely.

They are not known in current demon civilization except through
distorted myths and legends: - "Sky Architects" - "Star Builders" -
Forgotten pre-demon structural myths

These stories are dismissed as fiction.

The word "angel" does not exist in modern demon understanding.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# III. THERYN

## The Crossing

Theryn, a powerful boundary walker, accidentally crossed into the sealed
realm through a flaw in reality.

She did not break the seal. She slipped through a structural weakness.

Time moved slower there. She remained for what felt like years (from the
angel realm perspective).

She met one of the surviving angels. They fell in love.

Eventually she found a way back to the demon world.

She did not initially realize she was pregnant.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# IV. RACHEL'S BIRTH

Rachel is the biological child of:

-   Theryn (demon boundary walker)
-   A surviving angel (name TBD)

Rachel is: - Half demon - Half angel - Structurally unique - Possibly
the only hybrid in existence

As an infant:

-   Boundaries thinned around her
-   Wards responded unpredictably
-   Geometry realigned in her presence
-   Architecture resonated with her

Theryn realized Rachel was not just powerful.

She was structurally disruptive.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# V. WHY THERYN SENT RACHEL TO EARTH

Earth is:

-   Structurally inert
-   Magically disconnected
-   Outside sealed cosmological architecture
-   Stable and low resonance

Rachel's presence would not destabilize the seal there.

Theryn sent Rachel to Earth deliberately to protect her from:

-   The Demon Council
-   Political weaponization
-   Vex
-   Structural detection from the sealed realm

Theryn knew she had a child. She chose to hide her.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# VI. THERYN'S CURRENT STATUS

Theryn is alive.

After sending Rachel away, she eventually began traveling realms again.

Her goal:

To find the sealed angel realm again. To find the man she loved. To
understand what Rachel is.

She is walking thin places across realms.

She does not know Rachel was summoned back.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# VII. RACHEL'S ABILITY DIFFERENTIATION

Rachel's powers differ from demons in key ways:

Demons: - Reinforce boundaries - Seal fractures - Break through
thickness - Manipulate from within structure

Rachel: - Realigns geometry - Redirects force instead of blocking it -
Edits structure - Feels architectural resonance - Experiences time
dilation near thin places - Crosses realms without destabilization

Her power is structural, not territorial.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# IX. RACHEL'S FATHER

He is alive.

From his perspective: - Only \~2 years have passed since Theryn left. -
He believed she would return. - He never stopped hoping.

He recognizes Rachel instinctively via family resonance.

He does NOT call her daughter immediately. He first asks:

"Where is she?"

Hope defines him.

# XI. SEAL STABILITY RULE

Rachel does not shatter the seal.

Instead:

-   A small aperture responds only to her resonance.
-   The seal remains intact.
-   Each crossing subtly changes equilibrium.

The wrongness remains contained --- for now.
